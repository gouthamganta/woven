# Woven — First Time User Guide

Welcome to Woven. This guide walks you through everything you'll encounter in your first days using the app — what each screen does, what the choices mean, and what to expect as you go.

---

## Before You Start: What Makes Woven Different

A few things work differently here than on other dating apps.

**You write a note before every choice.** When you find someone you want to connect with, you don't just tap a button. You write them a short opening note — between 20 and 150 characters — before your choice is submitted. That note is private until a mutual match exists, but it's required. There are no reflexive choices in Woven.

**You only see 5 profiles per day.** The daily deck is curated by Woven's matching engine, ECHO. Five is the limit. Once you've responded to five profiles (in any combination of choices), the day's deck is done. The choices are meant to feel like decisions, not a scroll.

**Woven never shows you compatibility scores.** You'll see a short explanation on each profile card — "What caught our eye" — but it will never say "85% compatible" or anything like that. The matching engine is invisible on purpose.

**Sparks are your soft currency.** You start with 5 sparks per day, and can hold up to 10. You spend sparks when you respond to people in the Drawn tab — people who already chose you. You don't spend sparks in the regular deck.

## Step 1: Onboarding

When you first open Woven, you'll go through a setup flow before reaching the main app. Each step is required.

### Welcome

A brief introduction. Nothing to fill in.

### Basics

You'll enter:
- Your first name
- Gender and pronouns
- Birthday
- City and state

### Intent

You'll choose your relationship intent (serious, casual, exploring, etc.) and write one sentence about what you're looking for. This reflection is encrypted — Woven does not display it to other users.

### Foundational Questions

This is the most important onboarding step. Woven's matching engine will generate a set of questions covering five dimensions of who you are. Your answers are used to build your initial compatibility profile — ECHO will use them to understand you from day one, before it has any behavioral data about you.

Answer honestly. These are not questions designed to make you look a certain way — they're questions ECHO uses to understand you, not to judge you.

### Photos

Upload your profile photos. You need at least one.

One thing to know: ECHO learns over time which of your photos resonates best with each individual viewer, based on what people have chosen before. So even if you upload multiple photos, different people may see different ones as your lead photo.

### Details

Optional fields like hobbies. Fill in what feels natural. Hobbies are used in match explanations and in the date ideas that appear later in conversations.

### Lifestyle

Diet, workout habits, children preference, drinking, smoking, religion. All optional, all used by ECHO for compatibility scoring.

### Review

A summary of your profile before you go live. Check it over. You can go back and edit.

### Start

You're in. The main app opens.

## Step 2: Your First Day in Moments

When you enter the main app, you'll land in Moments. This is where your daily deck lives.

### The Deck Tab

You'll see profile cards — up to 5 today. Each card shows:

- The person's **name**
- A **verification badge** (✓) if they've been verified
- **"What caught our eye"** — a short explanation with a headline and a few bullets about why ECHO surfaced this person for you
- Two choice buttons: **◈ Magical** and **◇ Resonant**
- A **— Pass** option

### What ◈ Magical and ◇ Resonant Mean

**◈ Magical** is for a full, instinctive connection — the kind where something just clicks.

**◇ Resonant** is for an intellectual or deeply aligned feeling — where you've read the profile and feel genuine recognition.

Both create a real, equal match if the other person responds positively. Neither is "more" than the other.

### What Happens When You Choose

When you tap ◈ Magical or ◇ Resonant, a small overlay appears. This is where you write your **opening note** — 20 to 150 characters.

A few starter phrases are available if you want a prompt:
- "Your photo made me want to say…"
- "I'd love to talk about…"
- "Something tells me we'd…"

You can use these as a starting point or write your own. The note has to be at least 20 characters, so give it a genuine sentence or two.

When you submit, your choice and note are saved. If the other person has already submitted their choice and note, you'll have a match immediately. If not, you're in a waiting state — you won't know whether they've seen your card yet.

### What Pass Does

Tapping — Pass records a silent negative signal. No notification is sent. No match check happens. It doesn't cost anything.

### After Your 5 Responses

Once you've responded to five profiles today (any combination of Pass, Magical, or Resonant), the deck is done. It refreshes tomorrow with a new set of candidates.

## Step 3: The Drawn Tab

The **Drawn** tab is the second tab in Moments. It shows people who have already chosen you positively in the last 7 days.

This is different from the Deck. These are real choices that real people submitted — not suggestions from ECHO.

### What You'll See

Each entry shows the person's profile and a countdown: "Xh left" or "Xd left." If you don't respond within 7 days, the entry expires.

### How to Respond

Tap ◈ Magical or ◇ Resonant. (There's no Pass in Drawn — if you don't want to respond, you just let the entry expire.)

You'll write an opening note, just like in the Deck.

**Important: responding to a Drawn entry costs 1 spark.** The spark is deducted when you submit your choice. If you're at 0 sparks, you can't respond — you'll need to wait for your daily refill (5 sparks, up to a 10-spark maximum).

## Step 4: Your First Match (Balloon)

When both you and another person have submitted a positive choice and an opening note, a **Balloon** is created. This is Woven's word for a match — the connection window between two of you.

You'll both be taken to a shared chat thread, and you'll see each other's opening notes for the first time.

### Match Types

Your match will be labeled:
- **PURE** — you both chose the same type (both ◈ Magical, or both ◇ Resonant)
- **EDGE** — you chose different types (one ◈, one ◇)

This is informational. It doesn't affect anything mechanically, but it's a small piece of context about how you each landed here.

### How Long the Balloon Lasts

7 days from creation. The countdown is visible in the chat thread. If neither of you has reached the Find Love stage and the balloon expires, the match closes.

## Step 5: The Chat Thread

Inside the chat thread, you have a full set of tools:

### Messaging

Type and send text messages. They load in chronological order, 50 at a time.

### Voice Notes

Hold the mic button to record. Up to 180 seconds. When your match listens to it all the way through, a signal is sent to ECHO (you won't see this happen). If both of you send voice notes, that's captured as a meaningful moment too.

### Reactions

Tap ◈ on any message or on the opening ChatNote to react. One reaction per message.

### Availability Signal

Tap the availability button to send your match a note like "I'm free: tonight after 8!" — up to 200 characters. This pushes a notification to your partner.

### Games

Two games are playable inside the thread:

**Know Me** — one person generates 3 questions about the other from their profile. The other answers. You get a score based on how well the questions were predicted. You can set the difficulty (Easy, Medium, Hard) and tone (Playful, Thoughtful, Balanced).

**Red Flag / Green Flag** — 3 statements about one of you are generated from their profile. The other person rates each one: green, yellow, red, or "depends." Then the target rates themselves. You get a score based on alignment. There's a 90-second timer. After the game, the AI generates a short insight about the two of you.

Both games are grounded in real profile data, not generic trivia.

### Nudges

Occasionally you'll see a conversation prompt from Woven — a nudge to help the conversation along. You can dismiss it for 48 hours.

### Close Gracefully

If you've decided this match isn't for you but you don't want to ghost, tap Close Gracefully. The match closes without a ghosting penalty.

## Step 6: The Trial Period

Some matches include a **Trial** — a 3-minute live window that starts when the second person opens the chat thread.

If your match is a Trial, here's what happens:

1. You open the thread → a timestamp is recorded
2. Your match opens the thread → another timestamp is recorded; the 3-minute timer starts
3. For 3 minutes, you can message freely
4. When the timer ends, a decision appears for both of you:

   - **CONTINUE** — I want to keep this conversation going
   - **END** — this isn't right for me (you'll pick a reason: no spark, wrong timing, not my type)
   - **BLOCK** — close and block immediately

If you both choose CONTINUE, the match keeps going.

If either of you chooses END, the match closes. If neither of you had sent any messages, you each get 0.5 sparks back.

BLOCK closes the match immediately and blocks the person. No refund.

---

## Step 7: Find Love

**Find Love** is the final stage of a match. It unlocks 5 minutes after both of you have sent at least one message.

When you first both send a message, you'll see a countdown timer in the thread. When 5 minutes pass, Find Love unlocks.

### What Appears

Three date ideas appear in the thread. They're short — under 15 words each. They cover different activity types (one active, one social, one casual). If you both mentioned similar hobbies, at least one idea will incorporate them.

Each idea has a **Plan It** button.

### What You Do

Tap Plan It on the idea you like best. You'll see "Waiting to see if they're in…" until your match also taps.

When both of you have selected, venue suggestions unlock for whichever idea you both chose.

---

## Step 8: Commons

The **Commons** tab is the content feed. Users post tiles — photos, videos, text, voice recordings — that appear in other users' feeds. Tiles expire after about 7 days.

### Browsing

Scroll through tiles from other users. Your energy meter tracks how many you've viewed today.

### Orbiting

If a tile resonates with you romantically — tap ◈ to Orbit it. This sends a direct signal to the tile's owner. It also feeds into ECHO's matching for the two of you, which may surface them on your deck in the future.

This is the most direct signal you can send to someone outside of the match flow.

### Posting Your Own Tiles

You can post your own tiles (photos, videos, text, voice). Tiles go through a moderation queue before appearing in feeds.

## Things to Know About Woven

**Sparks refill daily.** You get 5 per day, held in a wallet with a max of 10. Plan them accordingly if you have Drawn entries expiring soon.

**Ghost refunds are partial.** If a match closes before either of you sends a message, you each get 0.5 sparks back. It's not a full refund — it's a small acknowledgment that you both showed up.

**ECHO learns from everything.** Every choice, every voice note you listen to, every trial you continue — ECHO is learning what real connection looks like for you specifically. Your deck improves over time as this data accumulates.

**You never see a compatibility score.** The "What caught our eye" section is the most you'll ever see about why ECHO chose someone for you. Trust it, and trust the note you write.

**Age is not shown on deck cards.** You'll see name, badge, and the match explanation — not age.
