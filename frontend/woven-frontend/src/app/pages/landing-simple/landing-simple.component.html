Meet ECHO. It knows the difference between your type and your person.

Every app has an algorithm. Theirs learns which faces you swipe on. That's it.

ECHO learns something harder: the gap between what you say you want and who you actually connect with. You say "calm and settled" — but your best conversations are with the chaotic ones. You say age matters — your patterns say it doesn't. Your checklist lies to you. ECHO doesn't take it personally. It just pays attention.

It reads you across eight things that actually predict connection — your lifestyle, your energy, how you communicate, how you show care, what steadies you, what you value, what makes you curious, and your emotional rhythm. Every match, every conversation, every Pulse teaches it. Tomorrow's five are chosen better than today's.

It's not an algorithm learning your type. It's a matchmaker learning you.

Every conversation ends honestly. That's a promise.

When you match, you get a Balloon — it opens with both your notes pinned at the top. You already said something real to each other. Start from there.

The Balloon lives for 36 hours. Both of you send real messages → 5-minute gated window opens. An intense, contained moment. Then one of you pops it — either as the natural end or as the exit ramp ("not feeling it? pop it, no explanation needed").

Pop triggers 3 minutes of reflection. Chat locks. You both sit with it. Then you both choose: Continue → Find Love unlocks (date ideas, games, the works). Exit → clean ending. No ghosting possible. Leaving well isn't rude here. It's the rule.
