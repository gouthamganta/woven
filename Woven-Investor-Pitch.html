How Mapping Helps

Pillars capture behavioral tendencies across 8 dimensions. Each user gets a pillar score (0.0-1.0) derived from foundational questions + behavioral signals. ECHO finds users with compatible patterns, not identical ones.

Why It Works

Your stated preference: "I want calm and settled." Your actual pattern: best conversations with chaotic energy. ECHO learns the gap between what you say and who you connect with. Your checklist lies. ECHO doesn't.

Pillar component weight: 0.19 (highest in 16-component system). Combined with Intent (0.12), Expression (0.09), Visual (0.10), and 12 other signals to create final match score. Weights personalized per user after ≥5 connection outcomes.

Product

Every feature feeds ECHO.
It's a closed-loop learning system.

Daily Pulse → ECHO Input

Morning check-in: energy, tone, openness. ECHO adjusts deck based on TODAY's state (not yesterday's). Pulse component weight: 0.06.

Five Moments → ECHO Output

Curated by ECHO based on YOUR vector + behavioral match. Each person: Photo (CLIP-matched) + ECHO voice intro + match explanation. Scarcity = intention.

Balloon (36h) → Signal Capture

Gated conversation window. ECHO captures: TimeToFirstMessage, ChatDepth, VoiceExchange, TrialDecision. No ghosting possible (forced Continue/Exit decision). Trial outcomes feed weight learning.

Find Love → ECHO-Powered Services

Date ideas (personalized), Know Me game, Plan a Date (real venues). Unlocks after Continue decision. ECHO tracks DateIdeaAccepted as outcome signal.

Commons → Collaborative Signals

Community feed. Orbit system (likes on tiles). CfScore (collaborative filtering) learns shared interests via Jaccard similarity. SharedTileAffinity component: 0.05.
